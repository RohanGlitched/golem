Hover

A Skydio X2 quadrotor holds a point 1.0 m above the floor. A cascaded PD controller turns position error into tilt, and tilt into the thrust of four rotors.

Drag a number in a sentence, or shove the drone: a gust pushes it sideways and the controller leans into the wind to come back.

Which lines are expected to break

Line 1.3 asks the drone to stay within 1.0 m in a gust of up to 20 N. The controller leans at most 35 degrees, so from about 10 N the gust carries the drone out of that ball. Line 3.2 asks it to cope with a position estimate that lags by up to 0.30 s. Its position loop oscillates from a lag of about 0.27 s. A broken line is a valid result and the line is never edited to avoid it.

What "from any direction" means here

A gust comes from a compass direction, counterclockwise from +x seen from above. The force points away from that side. A "held" result means no counterexample was found within the search budget, not a proof.
