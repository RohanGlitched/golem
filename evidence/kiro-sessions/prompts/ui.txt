Create a feature spec named golem-studio-ui for skeleton/src/ui, exported as golem-spec/ui: the shared studio both apps use. Read design-language.md closely; it is binding. Scope:

(1) Stage
- A plain three.js renderer that builds meshes from any MjModel. Geom types: mesh, box, sphere, capsule, cylinder, ellipsoid, plane. Visual group 2 only.
- A clay material: unfired clay under a single raking key light with a soft fill, and subtle grain from noise in the shader. No textures to download.
- A quiet floor, and camera orbit with damping.
- Render on demand: only while simulating, dragging or replaying.
- Cap devicePixelRatio at 1.5. Pause when the stage is off-screen (IntersectionObserver) or the tab is hidden.

(2) Live sim and Shove
- The hero robot simulates in real time on the main thread, with a fixed-step accumulator so sim and render are decoupled.
- Shove: pointer down on a robot body, then drag. This applies a spring force from the grab point toward the pointer ray on a vertical plane, shown as an arrow with a live label in newtons.
- Touch support.
- Keyboard alternative: focus the stage, and the arrow keys push with 100 N for 0.2 s, shown the same way.

(3) Ghost swarm
- Up to 64 ghosts, rendered with one InstancedMesh per robot geom.
- A translucent fresnel-rim shader in the --ghost colour: additive blending, depthWrite off.
- Ghosts play recorded TrialResult poses in sync, generation by generation, from the falsifier's progress callback.
- Each ghost's push shows as a thin arrow at its trigger time.
- A ghost whose robustness goes negative turns --break at the time of its worst margin, then fades out over 600 ms. Survivors stay pale.
- Summoning animation: ghosts rise from the floor over 400 ms. Skip it under prefers-reduced-motion.

(4) Aura
- A floor decal under the robot, drawn from trial results.
- For each of 36 direction bins, track the largest force that held and the smallest that broke.
- Render a soft verdigris region inside the boundary, and cinnabar ticks where breaks happened.
- Update as trials arrive.
- Add an accessible text summary: the weakest direction and its boundary force.

(5) Tablet (React 18)
- The app's requirements as sentences, with EARS keywords styled per design-language.md.
- Every quantity is a scrubbable number control: drag horizontally, arrow keys step it up and down, or type a value. Set aria-valuenow and the related attributes.
- Editing a line in a text field recompiles it, with inline diagnostics from golem-ears underlined at the right column.
- Per-line status:
  - untested;
  - running, with a live trial count;
  - held, with the margin in units;
  - broken, with the shrunk counterexample summary, e.g. "falls from 168 N at 135 deg, 62% into the stride".
- A hairline fracture drawn through broken lines.
- A "Summon 64 ghosts" button on each line, and one for all lines.

(6) Reel
- For the selected broken requirement: a filmstrip of 8 frames around the worst moment, rendered from the replay.
- The shrink summary.
- Buttons:
  - "Replay the fall": slow motion on the main stage, with a scrubber;
  - "Copy seed": as JSON;
  - "Pin seed": saved in the browser's localStorage.
- A shareable URL hash that encodes the edited requirement text and the seed.

(7) Verdict panel
- Shows trials, the random vs guided split, the closest call, the 95% failure-rate bound, wall time and pool size.
- All numbers use tabular figures and animate gently.

(8) Layout, per design-language.md
- Desktop: a 380 px tablet column to the left of a full-bleed stage, with the reel under the stage.
- Phones: the stage at 56vh, primary actions directly under it, then the tablet, then the reel. No sideways scroll at 375 px.
- Light and dark themes via tokens, with a toggle that respects prefers-color-scheme.
- Visible focus rings, and aria-live announcements for verdicts.

(9) Entry point
- A mountStudio(el, app, requirementsMarkdown, options) function, so each app page is only a few lines.

Performance
- 60 fps with 64 ghosts on integrated graphics, measured with a rAF probe in a Playwright test.
- First meaningful frame in under 4 s on a mid-range laptop.
