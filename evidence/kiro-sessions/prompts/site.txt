Create a feature spec named golem-site for site/: the public website. It is a Vite multi-page static build that also bundles the two app studios at /stagger/ and /candy/, deployable as static files. design-language.md is binding: lab-notebook pages, a left-aligned single column at most 72ch, full-bleed figures, B612 / B612 Mono / Gloock (self-hosted woff2), dark and light themes. Every page runs from top to a rich footer, never ending quickly. Pages:

(1) / (home)
- The hero IS a live studio: the G1 walking on the stage with three of its requirements on the tablet. The first instruction is "Shove it" (drag the robot), and a button summons the ghosts. There is no headline-over-image hero.
- Then these sections:
  - the problem: robot controllers, increasingly written by AI agents, ship with demo videos instead of evidence tied to requirements;
  - how GOLEM works, as an annotated sequence: EARS line → scenario space and monitor → ghosts (random, guided) → shrink → pinned seed → the agent Stop gate. Each step has a small live or recorded figure;
  - the two apps, each with a looping recorded replay and a link to its studio;
  - measured evidence: headline numbers from evidence/, linking to /evidence;
  - the Kiro kit in 60 seconds (`npx golem-spec init` and what lands in .kiro);
  - limits stated plainly;
  - a closing call to action.
- Footer: sitemap, credits and licences (Unitree BSD-3, Menagerie Apache-2.0, MuJoCo Apache-2.0), changelog, repo link.

(2) /kit
- Docs for the CLI, the MCP tools, the hooks, agents, skills, power, workflow and Crew app, each with a copyable snippet and what it does in one sentence.
- The EARS dialect reference generated from the lexicons of both apps: every phrase template and its units.

(3) /evidence
- The falsifier benchmark (guided vs random), the G1 stability map rendered as an interactive polar figure, and the agent-gate A/B. Methods, budgets, pre-registration link, raw data links, and the limits of each result.

(4) /how-kiro
- How Kiro built GOLEM, generated from the repo where possible: the list of specs with requirement and task counts, steering files, hooks, agents, MCP servers, powers, skills, workflows, which Kiro surface did what, and honest notes on what went wrong.

Quality bar:
- Lighthouse performance ≥ 90 on / and /kit; no layout shift; no sideways scroll at 375 px.
- Every figure has a text alternative.
- Open Graph image and favicon.
- Playwright smoke tests at 1440, 390 and 375 px in both themes.
