Create a feature spec named golem-ears for skeleton/src/ears: the compiler that turns GOLEM-dialect EARS lines (see ears-for-robots.md) into CompiledRequirement objects (interfaces.md). Scope: tokenizer with source offsets; parser for the five EARS patterns plus requirement IDs; quantities with units and SI conversion; range phrases in triggers ('up to 200 N', '150 to 250 N', 'from any direction', 'from behind', 'at any point in its stride'); matching trigger and response phrases against the app Lexicon templates with typed slots; temporal wrappers ('for 3 s' = always over window, 'within 6 s' = eventually within window, WHILE = min over t of max(-state, response)), anchored at the trigger event time in the Trace; robustness (quantitative STL) monitors returning margin in physical units plus the time of the worst margin; diagnostics with line/column, kinds (PATTERN_MISMATCH, MISSING_UNIT, UNKNOWN_PHRASE with the closest template by token edit distance, BAD_RANGE, UNKNOWN_ROBOT); a formatter (AST back to canonical text); and a function that extracts every EARS acceptance criterion from a Kiro requirements.md file (lines under '#### Acceptance Criteria', numbered like '1.' with requirement number context giving IDs like '2.1'), skipping non-EARS lines with a warning. Kiro writes criteria with a comma before THE ('WHEN x, THE robot SHALL y'): accept both with and without the comma. Property tests: round trip, unit identity, robustness soundness and sign flip, monotonicity, determinism, edit-distance metric laws.
