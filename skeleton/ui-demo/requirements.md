# Requirements: test pendulum

### Requirement 1: The bob recovers from pushes

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the bob is pushed with up to 15 N for 0.2 s from any direction THE robot SHALL keep the bob within 0.5 rad
2. WHEN the bob is pushed with up to 3 N for 0.2 s from any direction THE robot SHALL keep the bob within 0.5 rad

### Requirement 2: The box rests on the floor

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE robot SHALL keep the box above 0.05 m
2. WHEN the bob is pushed with up to 20 for 0.2 s from any direction THE robot SHALL keep the bob within 0.3 rad
